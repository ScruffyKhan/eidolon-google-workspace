Eidolon Google Workspace

Eidolon Google Workspace connects Elliot Carter's Google account to a personal AI assistant for owner-requested search, summarization and planning.

Read-only Google access

The integration requests read-only access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets. It can retrieve information for the account owner's tasks. It does not request permission to send email, edit or delete Google content, or share files.

Authorization and control

Google account access is granted through Google's OAuth consent screen, not through this website. This site contains no sign-in form and does not collect Google passwords, authorization codes or tokens. The account owner can revoke the connection in Google Account third-party connections.

This is a private integration for the operator's use, not a public registration service or a Google product.
